# Emberblood

A top-down, open-world action game you play in the browser. No install, no downloads.

**Play it:** https://muellercodes.github.io/emberblood/

## The story

A thousand years ago the dragon Vorlaxis, the First Flame, ruled the valley of Embermere. His daughter Ember loved the valley more than she feared him, so she joined five ordinary people (Iska, Bram, Oriel, Sedge and Tove) and together they split his fire in two. Half put him to sleep under Cinder Mountain. Half went into Ember, and it has passed down her family ever since. That family is the Emberbloods, and the five Ward-fires that keep the binding strong only relight with Emberblood fire.

You play Ash, the newest Emberblood: village lantern-lighter, part-time chimney sweep, and the first in a thousand years born with horns, a tail and real fire. It's the thousandth Lantern Day, the night the binding runs out. Kobolds just crashed the festival, somebody left the north gate open for them, and a cult called the Scaled Oath is snuffing the Wards so your great-great-great-great-great-great-great-great-great-great-great-great-great-grandfather wakes up strong and takes his fire back from you.

After Embermere the story keeps going for four more acts, each on its own map:

- **Act II, The Masked Thief**: someone stole the Ember Heart. Follow a brass gear down to the Clockwork Coast and Gearhaven, a city of stopped clocks, and work out who the Moth Mask is and why she only steals things that glow. New powers: Climbing Claws, Grapple Tail and Shadow Dash, plus the Chain Whip.
- **Act III, The Long Winter**: snow in June. Climb through frozen Frostreach to the dragon sleeping on Mount Dread, and find out who she really is. New powers: Blaze Breath, Wind Riding and Ground Pound, plus Blast Bombs.
- **Act IV, The Night Mother**: the moon is being eaten. Sail to Moonfall, dive the drowned city, burrow under the desert and race the eclipses to the last Shard. New powers: Dive, Burrow and Moonfire, plus the Moon Glaive.
- **The finale, Nightfall Peak**: climb the mountain that wasn't there, bring the friends you made along the way, and face Nyxara. Three endings, and the kindest one depends on what you did in every act.

- **The Case Book**: every act has mysteries to solve. You find the clues yourself by looking, following tracks and asking around, then work out the answer in the Case Book in the Journal (press B). A wrong guess gives a hint, never a fail.
- **Story Mode**: a 14,000 × 11,000 valley with seven regions, seven bosses and five Wards to relight. Seven towns and hamlets with about 45 houses you can walk into, and a dozen of them have an upstairs, a cellar or a secret room behind a loose brick, a bookcase or a painting. Seven caves built as small puzzle dungeons: push-block plates, crystal switches, torch-order murals, timed braziers, dark rooms, mazes, locked doors with small keys and cracked walls that hide treasure rooms.
- **Ember's Trials**: three big dungeons built by Ash's ancestor. Each has a gift you earn halfway through and need for the rest, a weapon in a big chest, a boss key and a boss: Old Gnarl the root giant (Trial of the Leap, you learn to **jump**), the Mirrow Serpent (Trial of the Tide, you learn to **walk on water**), and Skrae the Storm Wyvern, fought in the air (Trial of the Sky, you grow **wings and fly**). The new moves open up islands, broken bridges, lava islands and sky nests all over the valley.
- **Six weapons**: the Ember Blade, Twin Fangs (fast daggers, sneak attacks), a Pike that skewers a whole line, a Forge Hammer that smashes guards and cracked walls, the Ash Bow (shoot through your own fire breath for fire arrows) and the Wing-rang, a boomerang that stuns and fetches coins. Mom forges every one of them stronger with star-iron.
- **Skill tree and powers**: a skill point every level (up to level 25) and one more for each Trial, spent across four branches (Blade, Flame, Wings, Heart) of four rows each: whirlwind spins, fire arrows, longer flights, tougher scales and an upgrade for every power. Powers (jump, Water Stride, wings, Double Jump, Skimmer and the ones still out there) are found, never bought, and the Powers page in the Skills screen shows how to use each one.
- **Companions**: Pip the baby dragon, plus friends who follow you and fight: Duchess the goat (head-butts), Brisket the adventurer you thaw out of the ice, Juniper the mapmaker and, if you spared him, Greymane the wolf. Villagers notice what you've done and say so, and they chat with each other.
- **Side quests that play differently**: 27 of them. Chase down a purse thief, play hide and seek with the village kids, guard a pumpkin patch, race Elka through six flags, play back lost songs note by note, solve the case of the Mayor's missing pie (ask around, then accuse someone) and trade one wheel of cheese up into a piece of a fallen star.
- **People to talk to**: every named villager has a talk menu with chat, gifts, rumors and quest topics. How you answer (kind, brave or silly) changes how they feel about you, and friendship hearts open up new lines, discounts and favors.
- **Choices matter**: what you do with the High Priest, the lamp-maker who betrayed the village, the great wolf and the knight at the gate changes who helps you in the last fight and how the epilogue plays out. There are four endings, and one of them only opens up if you've been kind and paid attention to the old pages.
- **Quest journal**: track up to three quests at once on screen, pick which one the compass follows, and read the full log of active and finished quests, people, secrets and collectibles.
- **Three save slots**, each with its own chapter, playtime and level shown. Slots sync to the cloud when you're signed in.
- **Music**: a synthesized score that changes with the place and the moment (each town, the wilds, night, houses, the inn, caves and puzzle rooms, combat, bosses, the final fight), with little stingers for new quests, secrets and level-ups. Nothing is downloaded; it's all generated in the browser.
- **Looks 3D**: a toy diorama look in the style of the Link's Awakening remake. Every character (Ash, all the villagers, animals, enemies and bosses) is a little 3D toy figure that turns smoothly to face any direction, with walk cycles and a pose for every attack. Tilt-shift blur, trees and houses that lean with the camera, houses that hide whoever stands behind them, sun shadows that move through the day, and soft bloom. Settings has 3D depth (Off, Light, Full) and Characters (3D or Classic).
- **More story**: little adventures pop up while you explore (a wagon stuck in the mud, a kitten up a tree, falling stars, a ghost who lost his hat, a hungry mimic, a goat stampede, a frog choir and more), Tikka the runaway kobold baker has a story of her own across the chapters, and resting at a shrine or an inn can bring a dream from Ember's past.
- **Simple words**: Settings has Words: Full or Simple. Simple swaps the talks, quests, bubbles and messages for short versions in easy words for younger players.
- **Never miss a line**: messages stay up long enough to read and queue up instead of replacing each other, and the Log (a button on the dialogue box, the pause menu, or the Journal) keeps everything anyone said, every choice you picked and every message.
- **Arena**: endless waves in the Proving Grounds with every weapon, and a leaderboard. (Easy, Normal and Hard are the difficulty, picked on the title screen, for the story and the Arena alike.)

## Controls

| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Move (Ash faces the way he walks) |
| Shift | Sprint |
| Left click / J / Enter | Attack with the current weapon (it locks onto a foe in front of you). With no foe near, it talks, opens and uses instead |
| Right click / K | Fire breath (also lights lanterns and Wards) |
| Space | Dodge roll |
| C | Jump (press again in the air to fly, once you have wings) |
| X / Z / 1 to 6 | Next weapon / previous weapon / pick a weapon |
| G | Call your companion (twice: wait here) |
| U | Skills |
| Q | Dragon Roar |
| R | Healing draught |
| E | Talk, open, rest |
| M / Tab | Map and fast travel |
| I | Quest journal |
| T | Switch which tracked quest the compass follows |
| N | Mute or unmute |
| Esc / P | Pause and settings |
| L | In a conversation: open the Log |

Mouse aiming (Ash faces the pointer) is an option in Settings: Aim with the mouse. Settings also has Handed: Left, which mirrors the touch buttons, switches the controller to southpaw, and shows a keyboard set for a mouse in the left hand (arrow keys move; Enter attacks or talks, Right Shift sprints, Right Ctrl rolls, / jumps, ' breathes fire, . drinks, ; roars, [ and ] switch weapons). Tips can be closed with their X or turned off in Settings.

| Controller (PlayStation, Xbox or Nintendo) | |
| --- | --- |
| Left stick | Move (push all the way to sprint, click to toggle sprint) |
| Right stick | Aim |
| Bottom button (A, ✕, B) | Jump, fly |
| Right button (B, ○, A) | Roll |
| Left button (X, □, Y) | Attack |
| Top button (Y, △, X) | Talk, open, use |
| RT / R2 and LT / L2 | Attack and fire breath |

Menus, dialogue and the skill tree all work with a controller too. On an Xbox the game runs in the Edge browser.

Touch controls appear automatically on phones and tablets. In Settings you can pick a two-thumb layout (stick on the left, a thumb arc of buttons on the right) or a one-handed layout for either hand, turn on auto-attack, and resize the buttons.

## Accounts and cloud saves

Saves always live in your browser. If you make an account, your story also syncs to the cloud so you can pick it up on another device, and your Arena best goes on the leaderboard.

The backend is a tiny [Cloudflare Worker](worker/src/index.js) with a [D1](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/) (SQLite) database. It fits in Cloudflare's free tier and, unlike some free databases, it doesn't pause or get deleted when nobody plays for a while. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256, sessions are random tokens stored only as hashes, and there's no email or personal info.

## Project layout

```
game/src/      game code, split into modules (concatenated in order by build.mjs)
game/story/    the story as data: chapters, side quests, talk, clues and the world registry, act by act
game/*.html    page markup and styles
tools/         the story checker, the story map, the world check and the old-text extractor
build.mjs      builds docs/index.html (and runs the story checker first)
docs/          the published site (GitHub Pages serves this folder)
worker/        Cloudflare Worker API + D1 schema
```

## Building

```bash
node build.mjs                       # uses game/config.json for the API address
API_BASE=http://127.0.0.1:8787 node build.mjs   # point at a local worker
```

Then open `docs/index.html`, or serve the folder (`npx serve docs`).

The story lives in `game/story/` as plain data that one engine (`game/src/e10_story.js`) runs. Every build checks it first and stops if the story and the world disagree: a place the story mentions that isn't on the map, a key that opens nothing, a step or ending that can't be reached with the powers you have by then, a scene that runs too long. The report goes to `dist/check.md`.

```bash
node tools/check.mjs            # the story check on its own (--act 2 for one act)
node tools/storymap.mjs         # dist/storymap.html: every act as a graph of chapters, clues, choices and endings
python3 tools/worldcheck.py     # boots the game headless and checks every place and gate is really there
```

## Running the backend yourself

```bash
cd worker
npm install
npx wrangler login
npx wrangler d1 create emberblood        # copy the database_id into wrangler.toml
npx wrangler d1 execute emberblood --remote --file=schema.sql
npx wrangler deploy
```

A database made from an older `schema.sql` needs the migrations in `worker/migrations/` once, in order (`npx wrangler d1 execute emberblood --remote --file=migrations/0001_admin.sql`).

Put the `*.workers.dev` URL it prints into `game/config.json`, rebuild, and push.

### Admin page

`docs/admin.html` (on the live site at `/emberblood/admin.html`) is for the accounts named in `ADMIN_USERS` in `wrangler.toml`. It lists every account with its journeys, last save and Arena scores, and lets an admin view, download or replace a save (the old one is kept as a backup, and backups can be restored), set a new password, pause or turn an account back on, delete an account, and "view as" any account. A view session is look-only: the server accepts its saves and scores but never writes them, it never renews, and it ends after 12 hours. Use it in a private window, because the game in that window loads the viewed account's journeys. The live game uses `https://emberblood-api.muellercodes.workers.dev`. Allowed browser origins are set by `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` in `wrangler.toml` (localhost is always allowed for testing).
