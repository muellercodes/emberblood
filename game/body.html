How to Play

Move and fight

WASD / ArrowsMove. Ash faces the way he walks. ShiftSprint (uses stamina) Left click / JAttack. Hold for a combo. Ash turns to the closest foe in front of him. Right click / KFire breath. Uses your fire meter. It finds the foe in front of you too. SpaceDodge roll. Nothing hits you mid-roll. CJump, once Ash learns how. With wings, jump again in the air to fly. QDragon Roar, once the meter is full RDrink a healing draught

Everything else

ETalk, open, rest at a shrine X / ZNext or previous weapon 1 to 6Pick a weapon GCall your companion USkills M / TabWorld map and fast travel IJournal TSwitch which quest you follow NSound on or off Esc / PPause LIn a conversation: the log of everything that was said

In menus the arrow keys move, Enter picks and Esc goes back. Like aiming with the mouse? Turn on Aim with the mouse in Settings.

Two thumbs

  • Drag on the left side to move. Push the stick all the way to sprint.
  • SLASH attacks. Hold it for a combo.
  • JUMP shows up once Ash learns to jump. With wings it says FLY in the air.
  • FIRE breathes fire. ROLL dodges. ROAR works when its ring is full.
  • DRINK heals. You can also tap the green flasks at the top.
  • WEAPON switches weapons once you have two or more.
  • USE pops up when you can talk, open something or go inside.
  • CALL calls your companion.

The screen

  • Tap the minimap for the world map. Tap the quest list for the journal.
  • Missed what someone said? Tap Log above the talking box. The pause menu has it too.
  • The pause button is at the top right.
  • One-hand mode in Settings puts every button under one thumb, and Ash swings by himself.
  • Settings can make the buttons bigger or smaller.

Playing in Edge on an Xbox? Hold the Menu button and pick Use game controls, so the stick moves Ash and not a mouse pointer. Settings has a fullscreen button for the TV.

New in this version

  • More weapons. Twin daggers, a spear, a forge hammer, a boomerang and a bow. Each one fights in its own way.
  • Ember's Trials. Three old dungeons built long ago by Ember, one of Ash's ancestors. Each one wakes a gift in your blood.
  • Jump. Win it in the Trial of the Leap in Whisperwood. Hop over gaps, fences and pits.
  • Water stride. The Trial of the Tide on Lake Mirrow. Walk right across the water.
  • Wings. The Trial of the Sky in the Drakeling Crags. Jump, then jump again in the air to fly.
  • Skills. Every level gives you a skill point. Spend it on Blade, Flame, Wings or Heart.
  • Companions. Friends can join you. They follow you, fight with you and chat. People in town notice what you do.
  • Controllers. PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo controllers work everywhere, even in menus. Settings can go fullscreen for a TV.

Surviving the wild

  • Follow the gold marker on the minimap and compass to your quest.
  • Rest at dragon shrines to heal, refill draughts and save. You can fast travel between shrines you have found.
  • Swings, rolls and sprinting cost stamina. Back off and let it refill.
  • Red shapes on the ground show where an attack will land.
  • Hold fire breath on lanterns and Ward stones to light them. Fire also melts snowmen, smokes out bees, and lights fireplaces.
  • A gold ! means someone has a quest for you. A blue ? means they're waiting for something you have.
  • Walk up to any door or cave and use it to go inside. Smash pots for coins.
  • Night is darker and wolves hunt. Chests hold experience for leveling up.
  • Bog water slows you down. Snow slows you a little too.
  • Slash a fire bolt to send it back. Knights block from the front.
