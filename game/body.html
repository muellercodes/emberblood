How to Play

Keyboard and mouse

WASD / ArrowsMove ShiftSprint (uses stamina) MouseAim Left click / JSword. Hold for a 3-hit combo. Right click / KFire breath. Uses your fire meter. SpaceDodge roll. Nothing hits you mid-roll. QDragon Roar, once the meter is full RDrink a healing draught ETalk, open, rest at a shrine M / TabWorld map and fast travel Esc / PPause

Touch

Drag on the left side to move. Push the stick all the way to sprint. Buttons on the right attack, breathe fire, roll and roar. Tap the minimap for the world map.

Surviving the wild

  • Follow the gold marker on the minimap and compass to your quest.
  • Rest at dragon shrines to heal, refill draughts and save. You can fast travel between shrines you have found.
  • Swings, rolls and sprinting cost stamina. Back off and let it refill.
  • Red shapes on the ground show where an attack will land.
  • Hold fire breath on lanterns and Ward stones to light them. Fire also melts snowmen, smokes out bees, and lights fireplaces.
  • A gold ! means someone has a quest for you. A blue ? means they're waiting for something you have.
  • Walk up to any door or cave and press E to go inside. Smash pots for coins.
  • Night is darker and wolves hunt. Chests hold experience for leveling up.
  • Bog water slows you down. Snow slows you a little too.
  • Slash a fire bolt to send it back. Knights block from the front.
